🔒 PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

Last updated: July 9, 2026 · Effective: July 9, 2026

📌 Overview

OtakuShelf ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free anime and manga tracking application built by Mansoor Satti. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using OtakuShelf, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. Ever.

📦 Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (used for login and sync verification)
  • Username (custom displayName chosen by you)
  • Account creation timestamp

App Usage Data

  • Anime and manga library list (titles, reading/watching status, progress details)
  • Episode and chapter progress tracking counters
  • Personal review notes written for specific titles
  • Vibe ratings submitted by you (Story, Visuals, Feels)
  • Pinned/favourited anime, manga, and light novels
  • Light novel volume lists and reading progress
  • Achievements and badges unlocked
  • OtakuWrapped performance stats and customized card state

Automatically Collected Data

  • Device model, manufacture info, and OS version (via Firebase)
  • App crash logs (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Banner ad interaction stats (via Google AdMob)
  • Anonymous app activity statistics and screen transitions (via Firebase Analytics)

🎯 How We Use Your Data

  • To initialize and sync your personal catalog, review notes, and progress states across devices
  • To calculate and compile anonymous community rating scores (Vibe averages)
  • To generate your personal metrics and custom OtakuWrapped statistics card
  • To deliver relevant banner advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To monitor, debug, and patch stability/crash issues using Crashlytics logs

🔥 Firebase & Google Services

OtakuShelf relies on Google Firebase platform configurations for core services. Your data is stored securely on Google's globally distributed cloud servers.

  • Firebase Auth — Authenticates sessions, manages credentials, and stores secure hashes.
  • Cloud Firestore — Stores user library catalogs, notes, rating tables, and unlocked achievements.
  • Google AdMob — Manages ad delivery matching Google's advertising policies.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics — Collects diagnostic data and anonymized user flows to assure high-stability builds.

Google's overall privacy controls apply to these operations: policies.google.com/privacy

📡 Third-Party APIs & External Links

We use and direct users to these external services:

  • Jikan API — Fetches public anime/manga metadata. No personal user variables are sent.
  • YouTube — Direct links to anime/manga trailers which open inside the official YouTube client.
  • Legal Streaming & Reading Platforms — "Where to Watch/Read" links opening external platforms (Crunchyroll, Netflix, BookWalker, etc.). No personal profile details are shared.
  • Google Favicon Service — Fetches icons matching publisher websites. Only the site domain is sent to Google.

🧒 Children's Privacy

OtakuShelf is designed for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not intentionally acquire information from minors. If a parent is aware of registration of a minor, please contact us immediately to purge the record.

🌍 Data Retention

Your library tracking documents are kept live as long as you maintain your account. You can permanently request data purging. Consult our Data Deletion page for instructions.

🔐 Your Rights

  • Access — Inspect your current library and profile parameters.
  • Correction — Edit your username or adjust catalog values.
  • Deletion — Erase account and all related database entries completely.
  • Portability — Export catalog items to share or backup.
