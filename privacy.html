🔒 PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

Last updated: June 1, 2026 · Effective: June 1, 2026

📌 Overview

OtakuShelf ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free anime and manga tracking application built by Mansoor Satti. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using OtakuShelf, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. Ever.

📦 Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (used for login)
  • Username (chosen by you)
  • Account creation date

App Usage Data

  • Anime and manga library (titles, status, progress)
  • Episode and chapter progress tracking
  • Personal notes you write for titles
  • Vibe ratings you submit (Story, Visuals, Feels)
  • Favourite titles you pin
  • Light novel reading list and volume progress
  • Achievements you unlock
  • OtakuWrapped stats card data

Automatically Collected

  • Device type and operating system (via Firebase)
  • App crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Ad interaction data (via Google AdMob)

🎯 How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the app's core features
  • To sync your library and ratings across sessions
  • To display community ratings built from anonymized votes
  • To generate your personal stats and OtakuWrapped card
  • To serve relevant ads through Google AdMob
  • To improve app performance using crash reports

🔥 Firebase & Google Services

OtakuShelf uses Google Firebase for authentication and data storage. Your data is stored securely on Google's servers.

  • Firebase Auth — handles login, password hashing
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your library, notes, ratings
  • Google AdMob — serves banner advertisements

Google's privacy policy applies to their services: policies.google.com/privacy

📡 Third-Party APIs & Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Jikan API — Fetches anime/manga data from MyAnimeList. No personal data is sent.
  • Google Firebase — Authentication, database, crash reporting.
  • Google AdMob — Banner advertisements. Subject to Google's ad policies.
  • YouTube — Trailer links open in the YouTube app or browser. No data is shared.
  • Streaming platforms — "Where to Watch" links open external apps (Crunchyroll, Netflix etc). We do not share data with these services.
  • Manga reading sites — "Read Legally" links open external sites. We do not share data with these services.
  • Novel publishers — Publisher links open external sites. We do not share data.
  • Google Favicon Service — Used to display platform logos. Domain names of streaming/reading sites are sent to Google's servers.

🧒 Children's Privacy

OtakuShelf is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

🌍 Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. See our Data Deletion page for instructions.

🔐 Your Rights

  • Access — Request a copy of your stored data
  • Correction — Update incorrect information
  • Deletion — Delete your account and all data
  • Portability — Export your library data
