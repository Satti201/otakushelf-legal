🗑️ DATA DELETION

Delete Your Data

Your right to be forgotten — we take it seriously.

📌 What Gets Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data is permanently removed from our servers:

  • Your account (email, username, password hash)
  • Your entire anime and manga library
  • All personal notes you have written
  • All ratings you have submitted
  • Your achievements and stats
  • Your OtakuWrapped data

⚠️ This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Your community rating contributions will be removed from averages. This process takes up to 30 days.

📱 Method 1 — Delete from App (Recommended)

1

Open OtakuShelf

Launch the app and make sure you are logged in to the account you want to delete.

2

Go to Profile

Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.

3

Scroll to Account Section

Scroll down to find the Account section near the bottom of the profile screen.

4

Tap "Delete Account"

Tap the red "Delete Account" option. You will be asked to confirm. Once confirmed, deletion begins immediately.

📧 Method 2 — Email Request

If you cannot access the app, send an email request to us and we will process it manually within 5 business days.

Send your deletion request to:


delete@otakushelf.app


Please include the email address associated with your account. We may ask for verification before proceeding.

⏱️ Processing Time

Account deletion is processed as follows:

  • Immediate — Account disabled, login no longer works
  • Within 7 days — Library, notes, ratings removed from active databases
  • Within 30 days — All data purged from backups
