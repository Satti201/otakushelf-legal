🗑️ DATA DELETION

Delete Your Data

Your right to be forgotten — we take it seriously.

📌 What Gets Purged

When you delete your OtakuShelf account, the following items are permanently removed from our servers:

  • Your account credentials (email address, displayName, secure password hash)
  • Your complete catalog tracking list (anime, manga, and light novels)
  • All personal review notes and rating records
  • Your customized statistics dashboard and unlocked achievements
  • Your computed OtakuWrapped state card data
⚠️ Warning: Deletion is irreversible. Once processed, library data cannot be recovered. Community rating contributions will be subtracted from averages immediately. The complete purge from backups takes up to 30 days.

📱 Method 1 — Delete from Within the App (Recommended)

1

Open OtakuShelf

Launch the app on your device and ensure you are logged into the profile you want to delete.

2

Navigate to Profile

Tap the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar to open your dashboard.

3

Locate Account Options

Scroll down to the bottom of the profile screen to locate the Account action lists.

4

Confirm Deletion

Tap the red Delete Account option. You will be prompted with a final verification box. Confirm to begin the deletion process instantly.

📧 Method 2 — Manual Request via Email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, send us a manual request and we will disable and purge your data manually.

Send your deletion request to:


lightningdragon116@gmail.com

⏱️ Processing Timelines

Purging requests proceed through the following phases:

  • Immediate — Your account is disabled and access tokens are blacklisted.
  • Within 7 Days — Library catalogs, reviews, and ratings tables are deleted from active databases.
  • Within 30 Days — Backups and server cache nodes are fully purged.
